## Current economic branch

A working concept called the **liminal pulse / Flow-State Heart** treats circulation rather than a new standalone currency as the primary object. Existing domains may include fiat, cryptocurrency, game currency, goods, labor, knowledge, ecological capacity, debt, and verified impact.

Current exploration: regulate circulation of value and obligations; identify stagnation, concentration, starvation, depletion, and regenerative capacity; preserve reciprocal benefit; and test whether natural-system control analogies produce useful economic control laws.

A key derived idea is asymmetric utility: a contribution can impose low marginal burden on its source while removing a large burden elsewhere. Reciprocity therefore need not mean equal payment or identical commodities.

Nested incentives must be observed at the process level so that secondary incentives do not overpower the shared objective. The process itself produces residue.

Game currency is being explored as potentially exchangeable with external economic value, but no fixed exchange law, token, or claim of stabilization has been established.
